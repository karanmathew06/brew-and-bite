OUR STORY

Small breaks.
Big connections.

Brew & Bite is a fictional café concept imagined for busy student days in Bengaluru. Between lectures, deadlines and commutes, we wanted a place where a quick coffee could turn into a proper pause.

Our imagined menu pairs familiar brews with comforting bites, while our welcoming space makes room for quiet reading and lively conversations alike.

This website was created for a Marketing Analytics assignment to explore website visits and user interactions.

01 / MISSION

Make breaks better.

Bring enjoyable coffee, comforting food and warm service within easy reach of students.

02 / VISION

A favourite local corner.

Become the neighbourhood place people imagine returning to for everyday connection.

03 / OUR DIFFERENCE

Good taste. Fair prices.

A focused menu, student-friendly pricing and a relaxed atmosphere designed around everyday visits.
